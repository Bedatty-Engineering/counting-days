# Seasonal image sources

These locally bundled Christmas PNGs are from ClipSafari and are marked CC0:

- `christmas-lights.png`: [String of Christmas Lights](https://www.clipsafari.com/clips/o205912-string-of-christmas-lights), by finchweb.
- `christmas-tree-with-presents.png`: [Christmas Tree With Presents](https://www.clipsafari.com/clips/o245219-christmas-tree-with-presents), by GDJ.
- `colorful-presents.png`: [Colorful Presents](https://www.clipsafari.com/clips/o98539-colorful-presents), by kattekrab.

The [CC0 deed](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) dedicates the works to the public domain.

`christmas-lights-edge-to-edge.png` is a generated variation of the lights artwork, with smaller evenly spaced bulbs in a shallow strand for the page header.

`new-year-fireworks-cannon.png` is an original generated New Year illustration showing fireworks, launch rockets, and a cannon. It is bundled as the corner artwork for the New Year seasonal theme.

The following generated illustrations are bundled as corner artwork for their matching calendar holidays:

- `valentines-hearts-roses.png`: heart balloons, roses, and a gift.
- `halloween-pumpkins-cat.png`: jack-o'-lanterns, a cat, bats, and candy.
- `easter-bunny-eggs.png`: a rabbit, decorated eggs, flowers, and butterflies.
